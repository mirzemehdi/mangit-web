How the hybrid works

  1. Find the bubbles without a model

    A few milliseconds of image analysis find enclosed light regions with ink inside, their shape and which way the text runs. No AI call, nothing sent.

  2. Gemini Nano reads the easy ones

    Each bubble is cropped small and read on your computer through Firebase AI Logic. Horizontal text, Korean and Chinese, works well on-device.

  3. The cloud takes the hard ones, in parallel

    Vertical Japanese is Nano's weak spot, so those bubbles go straight to Gemini in the cloud while Nano keeps reading. Bubbles Nano is unsure about get a second opinion.

  4. Names stay consistent

    Character names you add are shared per series through Firestore and used by both models, so every reader of a series sees the same spelling.

Questions

Is Mangit Web free to use?

Yes. Mangit Web costs nothing and needs no account. It is open source under the Apache 2.0 license.

Does Mangit Web upload my manga pages?

Files you open are read in your browser and are never uploaded. In On-device mode, Gemini Nano reads every speech bubble on your computer and nothing leaves it. In Hybrid and Cloud mode, only small crops of speech bubbles are sent to Gemini in the cloud, never the whole page.

Which browsers does it work in?

Cloud mode works in any modern browser, including Safari, Firefox and Edge, on computers and phones. On-device and Hybrid mode need Gemini Nano, which desktop Chrome 139 or newer provides. If it is not available, Mangit Web asks before using the cloud.

What can I translate?

Japanese, Korean and Chinese manga, manhwa and manhua pages, into 14 languages including English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. Open a single image, or a whole chapter as a PDF, CBZ, CBR or EPUB file, and download the translated chapter as CBZ or PDF.

How accurate is it?

On our 15 test pages, Hybrid mode read 94% of the text correctly and kept 62% of speech bubbles on the reader's computer. Vertical Japanese is the hardest case for on-device AI, so Hybrid sends those bubbles to the cloud.

How is Mangit Web different from the Mangit app?

Mangit Web writes the translation over each speech bubble. The Mangit app for iPhone and Android redraws the whole page in your language, cleans the original text from the art and also translates sound effects.

Is there a Chrome extension?

Yes. Mangit for Chrome adds a side panel that translates the manga page you are reading on any website, with the same engine and the same privacy.

Mangit for Chrome

The Chrome extension adds a side panel that translates the manga page you're reading on any website, with the same engine.

  1. Download the latest mangit-lens.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Open a manga page, click the Mangit icon and press Translate this page (or Alt+T).

Privacy: Mangit Web counts anonymous totals (pages, modes, how many bubbles stayed on your computer) to show how it's used. No images, text, page addresses or names are ever stored.

Also on your phone. The Mangit app for iPhone and Android redraws whole chapters in your language, with cleaned art and translated sound effects.

Mangit Web is open source (Apache 2.0) on GitHub, built with Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference. By the team behind Mangit.
